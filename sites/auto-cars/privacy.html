Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Information We Collect

Auto & Cars collects minimal information necessary to operate the site. This includes:

  • Email addresses when you subscribe to our newsletter (via FormSubmit)
  • Anonymous usage data via Google Analytics (page views, session duration, referral sources)
  • Browser cookies for dark mode preference (stored locally on your device)

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Send newsletter updates about car launches and reviews (if you subscribed)
  • Understand how readers use our site to improve content
  • Ensure the site functions correctly across devices

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand site traffic. This service collects anonymised data such as pages visited, time on page, and general location. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-On.

Cookies

This site uses a single local storage key (theme) to remember your light/dark mode preference. No third-party tracking cookies are set by this site directly.

Newsletter

Newsletter emails are processed via FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). By subscribing, you agree to receive periodic emails from Auto & Cars. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email with "unsubscribe".

Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Email addresses are held by FormSubmit. Analytics data is retained by Google for 26 months by default.
