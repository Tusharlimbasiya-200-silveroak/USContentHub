Best Weekend Getaways Under $500 for Couples

📅 2026-05-26 ⏱️ 2 min read 📝 474 words

A romantic weekend doesn't require a $2,000 resort stay. Some of the best couples' getaways cost under $500 for the entire weekend — including lodging, food, and activities. Here are destinations and strategies for affordable romance.

Cabin Getaways ($150-300 for 2 nights)

Smoky Mountains, TN/NC: Cozy cabins with hot tubs, fireplaces, and mountain views start at $80/night on Airbnb and VRBO. Free national park access (Great Smoky Mountains has no entry fee). Cook dinner in the cabin and spend days hiking to waterfalls. Total weekend: $250-400.

Hocking Hills, Ohio: Ohio's hidden gem. Cabins nestled in forested hills with stunning waterfalls and caves. Cabin rentals from $100/night. Old Man's Cave and Ash Cave are free hikes. Wine tasting at local vineyards. Total: $250-350.

Big Bear Lake, California: Mountain lake town 2 hours from LA. Cabin rentals from $100/night. Kayaking, hiking, and a charming village with restaurants and shops. Total: $300-450.

City Weekends ($300-500)

Savannah, Georgia: One of America's most romantic cities. Walk along cobblestone streets under Spanish moss. Free attractions: Forsyth Park, River Street, historic squares. Budget hotels from $80/night. Southern food at Mrs. Wilkes ($25/person for a legendary family-style lunch). Total: $300-450.

San Antonio, Texas: The River Walk is free and gorgeous at night. Budget-friendly hotels downtown from $80/night. Visit the Alamo (free), eat Tex-Mex, and take a river boat cruise ($15/person). Total: $250-400.

Portland, Oregon: Food trucks, craft breweries, Powell's Books, and quirky neighborhoods. Budget hotels from $90/night. Spend nothing exploring neighborhoods and food truck pods. Multnomah Falls day trip is free. Total: $300-450.

Beach Weekends ($300-500)

Gulf Shores, Alabama: White sand beaches without the Florida prices. Condo rentals from $80/night (off-peak). Fresh seafood at The Hangout and LuLu's. Free beach access. Total: $250-400.

Outer Banks, North Carolina: Gorgeous barrier islands with quieter beaches than major resort towns. Off-season (spring/fall) cottage rentals from $100/night. Wild horses at Corolla, lighthouses, and fresh seafood. Total: $300-450.

Budget Tips for Couples' Weekends

  • Travel midweek if possible: Wednesday-Friday is significantly cheaper than Friday-Sunday at most destinations.
  • Book a place with a kitchen: Cooking 1-2 meals saves $40-80 over the weekend.
  • Pack picnic supplies: A grocery store picnic at a scenic spot is romantic AND free.
  • Focus on free activities: Hiking, beach walks, historic district walking tours, sunsets, stargazing — the most romantic activities are often free.
  • Drive within 3-4 hours: No flights needed. Gas for 300 miles round trip costs $30-45.
🎯 Key Takeaway: The best affordable couples' weekends combine a budget cabin or hotel ($80-150/night) with free activities (hiking, beach walks, historic neighborhoods). Smoky Mountains cabins with hot tubs start at $80/night, Savannah offers free cobblestone strolls under Spanish moss, and Gulf Shores has Florida-quality beaches at half the price. Book a place with a kitchen, pack picnic supplies, and focus on free outdoor activities — a romantic weekend for under $500 total is absolutely doable without sacrificing quality.
