The Best Free Things to Do in Every Major US City

📅 2026-05-25 ⏱️ 2 min read 📝 507 words

Every major American city has world-class free attractions hiding in plain sight. Here are the best free things to do in 10 cities.

Best Free Things to Do in New York City

  • Central Park — 843 acres of free green space, including the Bethesda Fountain and Bow Bridge
  • Staten Island Ferry — free ferry ride with Statue of Liberty views
  • Brooklyn Bridge walk — iconic 1.1-mile pedestrian walkway
  • Many museums have free hours: MoMA (Friday evenings), Met (pay-what-you-wish for NY residents)

Free Attractions in Washington DC

  • All 17 Smithsonian museums — free, including Air & Space, Natural History, and American History
  • National Mall monuments — Lincoln Memorial, WWII Memorial, Vietnam Veterans Memorial
  • Library of Congress — free tours of the most beautiful building in America

Top Free Activities in Chicago

  • Millennium Park — The Bean (Cloud Gate), Crown Fountain, free concerts in summer
  • Lincoln Park Zoo — one of the last free zoos in America
  • Lakefront Trail — 18 miles of free biking/walking along Lake Michigan
  • Art Institute of Chicago — free for Illinois residents on select days

Free Things to Do in Los Angeles

  • Getty Center — world-class art museum with free admission (parking $20)
  • Griffith Observatory — free entry, incredible LA views and telescope access
  • Santa Monica Pier — free to walk, people-watch, and enjoy ocean views
  • Venice Beach boardwalk — free street performers, art, and people-watching

Best Free Experiences in San Francisco

  • Golden Gate Bridge walk — 1.7 miles with stunning bay views
  • Fisherman's Wharf — free to walk and watch sea lions
  • Painted Ladies/Alamo Square — iconic Victorian houses and park
  • Fort Point — free historic fort under the Golden Gate Bridge

Free Things to Do in Boston

  • Freedom Trail — 2.5-mile walk through 16 historic sites (most are free to enter)
  • Boston Common and Public Garden — America's oldest park
  • Harvard Yard — walk through the famous campus for free
  • Institute of Contemporary Art — free Thursday evenings

Free Attractions and Activities in Nashville

  • Broadway honky-tonks — free live music all day and night (just buy a drink)
  • Tennessee State Capitol — free tours of one of the oldest state capitols
  • Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park — free outdoor history exhibits
  • First Saturday Art Crawl — free gallery walks downtown

Best Free Things to Do in Denver

  • Red Rocks Amphitheatre — free to visit during the day for hiking and views
  • Denver Art Museum — free for ages 18 and under, free first Saturdays
  • 16th Street Mall — free pedestrian mall with MallRide shuttle
  • City Park — free lake, views of the Rocky Mountains

Free Things to Do in Seattle

  • Pike Place Market — free to browse (the fish throwing is free entertainment)
  • Olympic Sculpture Park — free outdoor art museum on the waterfront
  • Gas Works Park — free park with Seattle skyline views
  • Pioneer Square — free walking tours on certain days

Free Attractions and Beaches in Miami

  • South Beach — free world-famous beach and Art Deco architecture
  • Wynwood Walls — free outdoor street art gallery
  • Bayfront Park — free downtown park with bay views
  • Little Havana — free to explore, Cuban coffee is $1-2
🎯 Key Takeaway: Every major US city has incredible free attractions. Washington DC leads with 17 free Smithsonian museums, but NYC, Chicago, LA, and San Francisco all have world-class free options. Plan your sightseeing around free attractions and save your budget for food and one paid experience. The best memories often come from walking through parks, neighborhoods, and markets — all free.
