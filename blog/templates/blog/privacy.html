Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide you with the best experience:

  • Newsletter subscriptions: Your email address when you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Comments: Your name and comment content when you post comments on articles.
  • Account data: Username and email when you create an account.
  • Analytics: Page views and article ratings (linked to IP address, not personal identity).
  • Cookies: Theme preference stored locally in your browser.

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver newsletter content you subscribed to.
  • To display comments and facilitate community discussion.
  • To prevent abuse through rate limiting.
  • To improve our content based on aggregate analytics.

Data Protection

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. All data is stored securely, and we implement industry-standard security measures including CSRF protection, XSS prevention, and secure headers.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Clear your local reading list and theme preferences through your browser.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.
